3. **Sour bite** (empty ground). Visual: first bite, face scrunch, in frame 1. Text: "creatine, but sour". Spoken: "This is what taking creatine looks like now." *A*
4. **Day 23** (from energyplus111's consistency turn, without his body claims). Text: "the hard part of creatine isn't the gym". Spoken: "It's remembering it on day 23." *A*
