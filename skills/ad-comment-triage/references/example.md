- **Escalate now:** 5 safety comments (kidney fears, bloating, a laxative effect, an "80g a day" question). None get medical answers; they get one approved holding line.
- **Decision:** stop running this post as your ad, or get a recut from the creator that shows your label serving. Replies can't undo what the video says. Meanwhile, answer the top dose comment once with your label serving.
