# Example: a finished Search campaign plan (booking app vs GlossGenius and Vagaro, 23 Nov 2026)

A real run of this skill (Agency mode, Standard, US) for an agency setting up a new client: a booking and payments app for solo estheticians and lash artists, $25 a month, 14-day free trial, $60 a day. No Google Ads account was connected, so Keyword Planner volumes are MISSING and nothing was created. Match its structure, its honesty about what was read and its depth. Its numbers are GlossGenius's and Vagaro's as of that day: never reuse them for another run.

---

*As of 23 Nov 2026, US. Read: GlossGenius's and Vagaro's Google ads (Ads Transparency, last 30 days), 7 of their Search ads opened as pictures (6 GlossGenius, each from a different launch date, 1 Vagaro), Similarweb for both sites. Not read: Keyword Planner volumes and bids (the client's account isn't connected), the client's own site. Competitors' spend, bids, keywords and results: MISSING.*

## 1. The verdict

- **The auction:** GlossGenius is the one running a conquest playbook on Search. Besides profession ads (/esthetician/software, /salon/software), it runs a **switching campaign** to lp.glossgenius.com/switchtoday ("Free transfer of client notes, appointments and services... Just $24/mo") and at least one **competitor-name ad** ("GlossGenius vs. Rosy"). Vagaro runs long-lived "Built for Salon & Spa Pros" ads to a free-trial page.
- **What that means for your client:** GlossGenius advertises "Starting at Only $24/month". At $25, price is not the angle. The angle neither competitor's ads use (in the 7 opened) is **the one-chair pro**: both sell "100+ features", "all-in-one", "Voted #1" to salon businesses.
- **Do this:** a non-brand campaign at $50 a day with 2 ad groups (estheticians, lash and brow artists), and a $10-a-day switchers campaign on "alternative" searches, **never on the bare names "vagaro" or "glossgenius"**. Their names are mostly typed by consumers booking an appointment and pros logging in.

## 3. The campaign

### Campaign A: "Search | Non-brand | Solo pros | US", $50 a day

| Ad group | Intent | Keywords (match) | Source | Volume / CPC | Landing page |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AG1 Esthetician software | High: buying software for their trade | "esthetician booking software", [esthetician software], "booking app for estheticians", "esthetician scheduling app" | GlossGenius's /esthetician/software ad group (observed) + client's audience | MISSING until Keyword Planner | A page for estheticians (MISSING: does the client have one?) |
| AG2 Lash and brow artists | High | "lash booking app", "lash tech booking software", "brow artist booking app", [lash artist scheduling app] | Client's audience; not seen in competitors' ads opened (a possible gap, Low confidence) | MISSING | A page for lash and brow artists |

**Held back:** "salon booking software" and "salon scheduling software". That's where GlossGenius's "Built for Salon Businesses" and Vagaro's "Salon & Spa Pros" ads compete, and a one-chair app shouldn't pay to show to salon owners with 10 chairs. Revisit once AG1 and AG2 produce trials.

**The ad-group ceiling** (H: about 10 clicks a day per ad group): ad groups = budget ÷ (10 × CPC). B2B CPCs here are MISSING; Keyword Planner gives them. At $50 a day, a $2.50 CPC allows 2 ad groups; a $5.00 CPC allows 1. If Keyword Planner shows CPCs above $5, run AG1 alone first.

### Campaign B: "Search | Switchers | US", $10 a day (own budget, so it can't drain Campaign A)

| Keywords (match) | Why |
|---|---|
| "glossgenius alternative", "vagaro alternative", "apps like glossgenius", "apps like vagaro", "switch from vagaro" | People already shopping away from them. GlossGenius runs this exact play against Rosy, with a dedicated switching page |

**Not bid on:** "vagaro" or "glossgenius" alone. "vagaro" has 58,640 searches a month, but Vagaro is a consumer booking site (14 million visits) and "vagaro login" (2,680) and "varago" (2,040) sit in its own top keywords: most of those searches are clients booking a haircut or pros logging in. "glossgenius" costs $9.65 and "gloss genius" $12.17 a click: at $10 a day that's about one click a day. Your ads may not use their trademarks in the text either.

Campaign B needs a switching page: what moves over, how long it takes, what it costs. GlossGenius offers a free transfer of client notes, appointments and services; if your client can't match that, say what it does offer, or hold Campaign B.

### Negative keywords (campaign level on both campaigns; each from the research)

| Negative | Match type | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "near me" | phrase | 4 of the 10 competitor keywords are "... near me" (hair salon, hair salons, barber shops, waxing) |
| "login", "sign in", "log in" | phrase | "vagaro login" 2,680 |
| "appointment", "book appointment" | phrase | Consumers booking a service |
| "barber", "barbershop" | phrase | "barber shops near me" 24,770: a different trade |
| "hair salon", "waxing" | phrase | Consumer services searches in the competitors' lists |
| "jobs", "hiring", "salary", "school", "course", "license" | phrase | Not buyers of software (general practice) |
| [vagaro], [glossgenius] | exact | The bare names: consumers booking and pros logging in. Exact keeps "vagaro alternative" open for Campaign B |

Each goes into `search_ad.negative_keywords` as `{ text, match_type }` (the tool defaults a bare negative to exact, which here would block almost nothing: an exact [near me] stops only the search "near me"). The create preview prints them as Google writes them (`"near me"`, `[vagaro]`); after creation, `get_my_keywords` with `negative: true` (free, synced about daily) confirms how they were stored.

## 5. When the client connects Google Ads

1. `check_my_ad_account` (free): billing, auto-tagging, and the conversion action for "trial started". Without it, the campaign can only be judged on clicks.
2. `get_my_keyword_ideas` (free): seeds from AG1, AG2 and Campaign B plus the client's pages, US. Fills every MISSING volume and CPC above, and sets the ad-group count by the formula.
3. `create_my_campaign` without confirm, one ad group per preview: Campaign A with AG1, then AG2 into it (`into_campaign_id`), then Campaign B. Each preview shows: creates, goal traffic, status paused, budget, headlines, descriptions, final URL, keywords with match types, negatives, locations, languages, and "Google checks Search campaigns when they're created; nothing is validated before." Each runs with `confirm: true` only on the client's yes.
4. `review_my_ad` (free) on each new ad, then the settings Adgence can't set yet (in Google Ads): bidding to Maximize clicks with a max CPC cap until about 15 to 30 trials a month (H), then Maximize conversions; Search Partners and Display off; location "Presence"; sitelinks.
5. Switching on is a separate preview and yes (`change_my_ads` resume).
