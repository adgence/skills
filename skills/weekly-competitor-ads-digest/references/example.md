## Baseline week, 17 to 23 Nov 2026, Meta, US

**Takeaway:** Arrae, the one rival here that sells *sour* creatine gummies, started Black Friday on 19 Nov at up to 40% off, and its Tone sale ad looks like their 93-day-old creator video with a sale sticker on it. Don't launch a full-price cold test this week; if you run a sale, put it on your best long-running video the same way, or hold new concepts until after Cyber Monday (30 Nov).

**Arrae: launching (Black Friday).** 990 live ads in the US (Meta's count); I read 120.
- 14 of the 120 read started since 16 Nov. 11 of them are sale ads launched 19 and 20 Nov ("Our biggest sale of the year is here", headline "ARRAE BLACK FRIDAY: UP TO 40% OFF SITEWIDE"), 9 sending people to their best-sellers page and 2 to the Tone variety pack ("My FAVORITE creatine is 40% off right now"). High confidence on the sale; observed.
- I watched the Tone sale ad ([1108958551970269](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1108958551970269), 3 copies): hands open a pouch of sour gummies in a gym, caption "I messed up", a "Black Friday sale / UP TO 40% OFF" box on screen the whole 25 s. Its length, 8 cuts and first cut at 3.7 s match their "I messed up" creator video already in your library ([1520225566815245](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1520225566815245), 93 days). So for the sale they reused the creative they've paid for longest rather than shooting new (inferred, Medium).
- 22 of the ads read crossed 90 days this week: the Tone launch batch of 22 Aug, still live. 12 are videos, 10 are catalog ads. The videos repeat two lines: "Creatine that tastes like candy??" (6) and "Can't decide on a favorite flavor?" (4), under headlines like "The Sour Way to Sculpt". That's their proven message, aimed at women ("sculpt lean muscle without the bloat or the bulk").
- 7 crossed 30 days this week (launched 19 to 23 Oct), all Tone except one hair ad, including a new landing page, /pages/shop-tone-vp ("Grow Your Booty, Without the Bloat").

**Crave Creatine: no clean read of this week.** 719 live ads in the US; the 120 I read lean old (newest started 9 Nov), so I can't say whether they launched anything this week, and I won't call them quiet. 4 ads crossed 30 days (22 Oct batch, all "Creatine is a natural fuel your body already produces...") and 4 crossed 90 days (22 to 24 Aug, the same education hook plus three creator lines such as "Try @cravecreatine !"). Their education hook keeps surviving.

**Bloom Nutrition: not advertising here.** Its page runs no US ads and 2 worldwide: Instagram-only images for "Bloom Sparkling Energy" pointing to a store locator, started 19 Nov. Nothing about creatine. Either they advertise through other pages or creators, or they aren't a Meta rival for you right now.

**Openings for you**
1. **Time it (High).** Arrae is at 40% off through at least Black Friday. A full-price cold concept test this week competes with their discount for the same buyer. Either answer with your own offer as an overlay on your longest-running video (judge it on purchases), or hold new concepts until 1 Dec.
2. **"Sour" is taken, the persona isn't (Medium).** 40 of the 120 Arrae ads read say "sour" and their winners speak to women who want to "sculpt" without bulk. Crave owns "tastes like candy". Your gap is the person neither speaks to: name who your sour gummy is for (lifters who hate powder? men?) before testing a sour taste hook against Arrae's.
3. **Watch next week, free.** If Arrae's Oct batch with the new /shop-tone-vp page survives to 60 days, that page is working for them.
