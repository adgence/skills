## 1. The verdict

- **Buyers love the feel, not the miracle:** soft, smooth skin (211 of 1,051 reviews), dry skin fixed (168), and "goes a long way" (58). Only 42 of 1,051 rated it 3 stars or lower, and half of those are about the order (damaged, opened, missing, wrong or "not the same" jars), not the skin.
- **What stops browsers is clogged pores:** 58 of 347 Reddit comments talk about clogging or breakouts, and 82 blame the other ingredients (beeswax, olive oil, essential oils) and ask for "just tallow".
- **This week:** make the pore objection the hook, answered by your ingredient list (only if it's short), with buyer words as proof: "a little goes a long way", "so soft". And fix the jar before you scale: melted and opened jars are your category's most common 1-star.

## 6. Your moves

| Decision | What | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pre-empt it** | Open on the pore fear and answer it with your ingredient count | 58 of 347 browsers; the 104-upvote "just tallow" comment | Medium |
| **Prove it** | Buyer words as proof: "goes a long way" (58), soft and smooth (211), "not greasy" (28), "absorbs" (36) | of 1,051 buyers | High |
| **Lead with it** | Dry skin at 40+ ("I'm 54", "at 56 hydration is huge") | 168 dry-skin reviews; ages named in the reviews read | Medium |
| **Fix, don't advertise** | Sealed jars, heat in transit, batch consistency | 21 of 42 low ratings are the order or the batch, and 13 of the 16 order complaints are on one listing (Lopeie): it's how a cheap seller ships, so beat it with packaging you can show, not with a category claim | Medium |
| **Avoid** | "Clears acne", "heals eczema", "anti-aging" as claims, "cow fat" | Health claims; browser disgust | n/a |

**Three angles to test** (purchases or CPA as the measure for each new angle; hook rate only for hook variants):

1. **"Just tallow" (pre-empt).** Hooks: visual, the jar's ingredient label held to camera; on-screen "2 ingredients. That's the whole label."; spoken "Everyone asks if tallow clogs pores. Here's what's actually in mine." *Needs your real ingredient list; skip it if yours has beeswax and five oils.*
2. **"A little goes a long way" (prove).** Visual, a pea-size scoop covering a whole face; on-screen "This is the whole amount"; spoken "Buyers keep saying the same thing: a little goes a long way."
3. **Dry skin at 50 (lead).** Visual, a woman 50+ touching her cheek; on-screen "Tried all kinds of moisturizers"; spoken, the customer's own line, read by a real customer of yours (never present these TikTok Shop reviewers as your customers).

## 7. The language bank

**Reuse (verbatim):** "a little goes a long way" · "so soft" · "not greasy" · "absorbs" · "my skin is so moisturized" · "just tallow and jojoba" · "I tried all kinds of moisturizers" · "whipped" · "unscented" · "my husband" (53 reviews: buyers buy for two).

**Avoid:** "clears acne", "heals eczema", "reverses wrinkles" (health and results claims) · "cow fat", "rendered" (browser disgust) · "chemical-free" (feeds the natural-versus-science fight in 33 comments).
