## 3. Brief for concept 1: "What a day of Crave costs"

**Concept overview:** a static and a 20-second selfie video that do the price math powder users do in their heads, but count the days they skip, because price is the most-raised objection in the market and nobody but Create (since 20 Nov) answers it.

**Copy, static (3 versions):**
- A: Headline "The real cost of creatine you actually take" · table: Powder $[X]/day, days you take it [Y of 7] (MISSING: your survey or a cited habit stat; if none, drop this row) · Crave $[Z]/day, 7 of 7 · CTA "Try it risk-free"
- B: Headline "$[Z] a day. Less than the coffee you take it with." (only if true) · CTA "See the math"
- C: Headline "Yes, powder is cheaper." · Sub "So is a gym membership you don't use." · CTA "Money back if you don't love it"

**Copy, video (20 s, founder or creator, one take):**
- 0:00 to 0:03: "Powder's cheaper. Until you count the days you skipped it." Text: "Powder is cheaper?"
- 0:03 to 0:09: "Here's what a day costs." Holds the bag, then the price tag. Text: "$[Z] a day" (MISSING)
- 0:09 to 0:15: "Five grams, lab-tested every batch, and you actually take it." Text: "Lab-tested every batch"
- 0:15 to 0:20: "And if you don't love it, you get your money back." Text: "Money back, keep the bag" (only if the guarantee still reads that way)

**Visual approach:** first frame is the two price tags side by side, no face; the face comes in at 0:03. Kitchen counter, daylight, captions word by word, no music under the voice.

**Deliverables:** 3 statics (1:1, 4:5), 1 video 9:16, 20 s, each opening line as its own file.

**Reference:** the side-by-side structure of your own "Which One Seems Easier To Stick With???" race (1390279629735742, 106 days, 6 copies): it already proved that a split you can read with the sound off works for you.

**Do not say:** Create, Arrae or any brand name; "cheaper than [brand]"; weight, GLP-1, menopause or brain claims; "clinically proven"; a habit statistic you can't cite.

**Success metrics:** read after 7 days or 2x target CPA per ad. Static: link CTR vs the 4.7-star batch. Video: hook rate vs the batch, then CPA. Hook rate low: change the first frame. Clicks but no sales: the landing page doesn't show the per-day price; add it above the fold.
